A trip to Maputo!



Where is Maputo?🌍

Maputo is the capital of Moçambique in southern Africa. It's a beautiful city on the coast of the Indian Ocean with delicious food, incredible culture, and a fascinating history.

What can I eat?


Maputo's most famous restaurant is Piri-Piri, a no frills establishment specializing in fresh sea food and world-renowned peri peri chicken. If you've ever been to a Nando's restaurant in the DMV area, you've likely had peri peri chicken before, but non can beat Piri-Piri's. On a four day trip, I ate at this reastaurant three days in a row. I reccomend the giant prawns!

Fun fact: the name Piri-Piri comes from the Swahili "piri piri", which means "bird's eye chilli pepper.

You can also enjoy plenty of Lunch Bars for dessert. They're a candy bar only available in southern Africa, and they're SO GOOD. My family is so addicted that we sometimes get a box of dozens shipped to us.
